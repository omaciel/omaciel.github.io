From The Frying Pan Into The Fire

Literally! Spent an agonizing sleep-less night after my surgery yesterday. My keloids were removed with a CO2 laser and whatever if left of my earlobes feel as if I had deep fried them in boiling sulfuric acid! Drinking an extremely cold Cappuccino Blast from Donkey Donuts helped a bit but later in the evening it was a mix of 2 extra stregth Tylenols + 1 Motrin that helped ease the pain. Got up early today (since I couldn’t really sleep, unable to shift from the only position I could be) and what a day it is! My weather applet on my laptop says it is 86 F right now. And the weather man says it will go up to the mid 90s! As funny as this may sound, I bet it is much, much nicer at work right now!
