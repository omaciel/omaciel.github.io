Helping the Belarusian and Esperanto Translations

Thanks to comments by booxter and Patrick Niedzielski, I have added support for 2 more languages to Pylyglot: Belarusian and Esperanto! So far I had been adding languages according to their translation percentage, starting from the top and slowly moving down the list, but it probably makes sense to add some of the languages that need a bit of help catching up with the other teams and provide them with a means to check their standardization. So I’m glad to welcome these two new “members” and kindly ask anyone who speaks either one of these languages to lend a helping hand with their translation by contacting their respective GNOME team coordinators:

  • Esperanto: Jacob Nordfalk
  • Belarusian: Ihar Hrachyshka
