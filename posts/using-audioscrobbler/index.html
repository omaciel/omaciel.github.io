Using Audioscrobbler

April 29, 2005

  • english
  • random
  • life

Read some posts of people talking about Audioscrobbler today and decided to see what the noise was all about.

Audioscrobbler builds a profile of your musical taste using a plugin for your media player (Winamp, iTunes, XMMS, foobar2000, etc..). Plugins send the name of every song you play to the Audioscrobbler server, which updates your musical profile with the new song. Every person with a plugin has their own page on this site which shows their listening statistics. The system automatically matches you to people with a similar music taste, and generates personalised recommendations.

Note that XMMS and foobar2000 are both supported! :)
